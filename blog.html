Apr 25, 2020

Update: Casa Cheerful Kindergarten

An update concerning the progress of the Casa Cheerful Kindergarten works: thanks to the generous support of Fabio Lehnert and Luis Ke (Hunderunde), Mirela could fence the whole plot and construct the main building with the exterior walls and the roof.

Still ahead at that point, for the main building:

  • Air ventilation and filtration system
  • Air conditioning and heating
  • Smoke detectors and emergency lighting
  • Flooring, ceiling and interior walls
  • Kitchen and wash facilities
  • Medical facility (second building)
  • Food and supply storage

A huge thank you from the deep of our hearts to our German friends from Hunderunde. ❤️
